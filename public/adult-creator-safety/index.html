Adult creator collaboration: safety and consent checklist

Professional adult-content production requires more than finding people to film with. The safest collaborations set explicit boundaries, clear permissions and reliable records before cameras roll.

Verify everyone participating

All participants must be adults and meet applicable legal eligibility requirements. Document identity checks and age verification using secure processes; do not rely on social-media badges as proof of legal capacity.

Different production formats and jurisdictions may impose additional recordkeeping, health and safety obligations. Obtain qualified advice when required.

Make consent specific and ongoing

Discuss the exact proposed activities, filming boundaries, location, participants and intended distribution. Written releases and agreements should reflect the actual production, not generic consent to anything.

Participants should have a clear way to raise concerns, stop participation and understand what can and cannot be withdrawn after distribution under the applicable agreement.

Protect sensitive material

Use restricted sharing for identity records, private galleries, release documents, unedited footage and conversations. Share media only with people who have the necessary rights and permission.

Do not include non-consenting people or identifying details about a private residence in public content. Report unauthorized uploads and privacy violations promptly.

Plan a controlled set and location

Confirm a permitted shoot location, realistic crew size, equipment, privacy measures and emergency contacts. Respect the host’s room restrictions, filming hours and property-access rules.
